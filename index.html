About this explorer

Gray arrows represent eligible H-neighborhood relationships from the exported 13B counts. They are not clinical associations, and may represent directions without any significant public clinical result. Colored arrows reflect clinical tests passing the current display filters. Nodes are original NMF components (nmf1–nmf23), not individual patients. A node with no primary patients can still be displayed. Arrows represent original-primary source patients whose operational H neighborhood includes a target component, compared against same-source patients whose H neighborhoods exclude that target.

Arrow color identifies a clinical covariate or family. Sex uses male blue / female pink; the 1031 protocol risk uses high risk deep red / low risk deep blue / unknown gray. Each color identifies the tested level, not an individual patient; solid/dashed indicates higher/lower prevalence (or median for age) among incoming patients, and width represents the number of incoming patients. BH FDR comes from Script 13B; all tested rows and eligible populations are preserved. A clinically significant bridge does not imply causality. For sex and risk, solid means that category is more common among incoming patients and dashed means less common. Female/Male tests can represent complementary descriptions of the same underlying comparison.

CpG counts come from Script 13C / 11D and refer to the source→target direction as a whole, not to an individual arrow's clinical feature. A physical CpG is counted once per direction, retaining overlapping primary-restricted and neighborhood-conserved membership. CpG selection and NMF were derived in the same methylation cohort, so the relationship is exploratory and not independent validation. Nearest genes are annotations, not proven regulatory targets.

Original-group covariates are imported separately from a reviewed one-vs-all group enrichment export; they are not inferred from incoming bridge associations. The node inspector shows reviewed one-vs-all categorical-variable tests, with descriptive category differences; each BH FDR is for the overall variable, not a post hoc category test. Risk means the 1031 protocol classification only.

Original pairwise survival comparisons are included in the node inspector as BH-adjusted chi-square survival tests from the pooled original-primary-group analysis. These are not Cox hazard ratios, do not give the direction of a survival difference, and are not survival tests of H-bridge subsets. The supplied table does not name the survival endpoint or exact test procedure; verify those details in the original analysis script. Kaplan–Meier links in node details show the original NMF-assignment survival plots when supplied by the site owner. They must not be treated as bridge-specific survival estimates. No OS/EFS comparisons are invented by the explorer.

This site is a static GitHub Pages explorer generated from aggregate-level files. It excludes USIs, patient-level methylation measurements, per-patient coordinates, and individual clinical records.
